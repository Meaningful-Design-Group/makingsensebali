Put a Bayu node on the map.

Four steps, one phone. Sign in, name the node, scan a code, wait for the first reading. This page has no server of its own: everything you type goes straight from your browser to api.smartcitizen.me.

1 · Sign in to Smart Citizen

No account yet? Create one on smartcitizen.me, then come back here.

2 · The node

-8.6500, 115.2100

3 · Hand the token to the node

······
  1. Plug in the node. On your phone, join the WiFi network that starts with MSB-Node- (password makingsense).
  2. Scan this code with the phone camera, or open the link below. The node saves the token.
  3. Pick your home WiFi, type its password, press Save.
Or open http://192.168.4.1/t?token=…
