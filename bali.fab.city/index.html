01 — Our signature measure

The Bali Fab City Index.

One simple number for a big question: how much of what Bali consumes can it also make and regenerate locally? The Index runs from 0 (imports almost everything) to 100 (locally productive and circular). It is how we hold ourselves to the Fab City pledge — 50% self-sufficiency by 2054.

Bali Fab City Index
v0.1 · developed with PlanetAI · illustrative v0.1→v0.2→v1

Today, like most islands, Bali imports most of what it consumes — food, materials, energy, products. The Index measures the share it could instead make, repair and regenerate close to home, using local skill, local materials and shared open designs. A higher score means a more self-reliant, more resilient island — and less carried in by ship and plane.

0
Imports almost everything. Production happens elsewhere; the island depends on what arrives.
100
Locally productive & circular. Most needs are met on-island; materials cycle, little is wasted.
50%

The Fab City pledge: every member commits to producing half of what it consumes by 2054. Bali's Index tracks the journey there.

03 — The network made tangible

What we do — Fab Lab Bali.

Bali Fab City grew out of a design practice, two convenings on the island, and a lab that now hosts the world's fabrication academies in Jimbaran. This is the network you can visit.

fablabbali.com ↗
  1. 2019

    Meaningful Design Group conceived

    The design practice (meaningful-design.org) that seeds the lab is conceptually created.

  2. 2022

    Bali Fab Fest + Fab Island Challenge

    Jimbaran. Local and global innovation communities convene to prototype meaningful interventions for Bali's emerging regenerative economy. Report ↗

  3. 2023

    Fab Lab Bali opens

    At Jimbaran HUB (J-Loft 2C, Jl. Karang Mas). Founded by Meaningful Design Group, Cast Foundation, Habibie Center & Jimbaran Hijau.

05 — How we measure it · PlanetAI

How the Index is computed.

The Index is built by PlanetAI, the research engine behind it. It reads Bali across five scales — from a single community to the whole planet — and it is sovereign by design: your data stays in Bali, and only an approved, anonymised summary is ever shared upward. Try the scales below.
