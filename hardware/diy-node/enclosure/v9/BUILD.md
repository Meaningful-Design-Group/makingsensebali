## What v9 changes (and why)

v8 printed and failed on two root causes (full write-up in `../HANDOFF_v9_redesign.md`):

1. **No board-access path.** v8 fused the hood to the body in one print, so the only
   openings were the Ø32.6 bayonet bore and the 30×28 gyroid window — both smaller than
   the 40×60 perfboard. The electronics had no way in.
2. **Floor bridge collapse.** The cavity floor was a ~43 mm unsupported bridge printed
   mid-body. PETG at that span failed; "0 supports" was never "printable."

v9 splits the enclosure into **three printed parts per variant**:

| Part | What it is | Fixes |
|---|---|---|
| **body** | Open-top **and** open-bottom hollow tube. All validated v8 internals carried verbatim. | Open top = boards load from above. Open bottom = no floor bridge. |
| **hood** | Separate domed crown, screws on with **2× M3** into top-rim bosses, prints flat-base-down. | Restores the DEC-03 crown release v8 sealed shut. |
| **base plate** | Full-footprint plate, prints **grille-down, flat on the bed, NO feet**. Doubled mesh-grille intake (~30×30, 2.5 mm floor) + 2 mm drop-in mesh/grid-panel seat, angled USB-C chase, weep. Mates by **press-fit spigot**. | Floor is now a bed-supported first layer, not a bridge. |
| **breathing plate** | Separate 35×35×7 insert (`tools/breathing_panel_v9.py` → `v9_breathing_panel.stl`): gyroid breathing field in a 30×30 vent, 2.5 mm solid border. **Slides down into a side rail and grips by friction.** | Serviceable breathing element; one plate fits both variants. |

> **Base rev2 (Tomas review, 2026-06-10):** feet removed — they hung below the grille and
> forced a feet-down print that lifts the grille ~3 mm off the bed and re-creates the v8
> unsupported-span failure. The node hangs on the wall via keyholes and never rests on
> feet, so the base prints grille-down with the grille as a fully supported first layer.
> Intake doubled (~20×22 → ~30×30, six 3 mm slots), grille floor taken to the full 2.5 mm
> wall thickness, and a real 2 mm seat added for a drop-in mesh/grid panel (serviced from
> inside when the base pops off).

Assembly: hang body on the wall via keyholes → load HM3301 down the rails (Plus) →
lower perfboard onto the back-wall standoffs from the open top → **slide the breathing
plate down into its side rail** → screw the hood on (2× M3) → press the base plate on
from below. Monthly service = pop the base plate off from below (mesh + USB), hood and
electronics undisturbed; the breathing plate slides out from the top with the hood off.

> **Breathing plate + slide-in rail (Tomas review, 2026-06-10):** the breathing-window
> insert is now a **35×35×7 plate** (was ~20×20×6.5) that **slides down into a vertical
> rail and grips by friction** (`COUPON_TBD_RAIL`), replacing the seat-against-a-ledge
> rebate. The rail mounts on the **outside** of the BME680 wall, in the rain-shadow:
> an inward mount leaves only ~1.5 mm to the Plus BME680 — too tight for the foil-liner
> air gap. C-channel per side (wall = inner stop, web = lateral stop, lip = outward
> retainer) + a self-supporting bottom locating ledge. One plate fits both variants;
> generated by `tools/breathing_panel_v9.py`. **Open item for the rail section test:**
> the rail mouth faces up — confirm it sheds water under the eave, or add a top rain-cap.

## The gate (unchanged from v8 — it's why v8 failed)

Nothing fit-critical prints before **ICD §5.1** is measured from the printed coupons
(`../coupons/`, README has the procedure). No full body prints before section tests.

### Section tests for v9, in order

1. **Press-fit ring first.** `COUPON_TBD_PRESSFIT` is the single parameter the whole
   base→body joint rides on, and press-fit is the most clearance-sensitive of the three
   attachment options. Print a short slice of the base spigot + a slice of the body
   bottom rim and dial the fit before anything else.
2. **Hood section** (lower ~20 mm + the M3 bosses) — confirm boss print quality and M3
   thread-forming in PETG. **Open item:** screw drive direction / access ergonomics. The
   flat back blocks rear access; deep service is off-wall via the open bottom. Geometry
   is staged as boss-pilot-on-body + clearance-on-hood-ear; the section test decides
   whether the screw drives up from the open bottom or the ear/boss roles flip.
3. **Base plate section** (the spigot + mesh grille zone) — confirm the grille holds the
   insect mesh and the USB-C chase clears the boot + bend radius.
4. **Breathing-plate rail section** — print a short slice of the side rail + a slice of
   the 35×35×7 plate edge; dial `COUPON_TBD_RAIL` so it slides in and grips by friction,
   and check the rail mouth sheds water (or add a top rain-cap).

## COUPON_TBD parameters in `enclosure_v9.py`

`COUPON_TBD_PRESSFIT` (base spigot vs cavity — staged +0.15, real value likely slightly
negative for an interference press, from the clearance ladder) · `COUPON_TBD_M3_PILOT`
(hood boss pilot, 2.5) · `COUPON_TBD_SLIDE` (HM rail gap) · `COUPON_TBD_PRESS` (pegs into
Ø3.2) · `COUPON_TBD_PILOT_M2` (standoff self-tap) · `COUPON_TBD_RAIL` (breathing plate in
its slide-in rail — staged 0.25) · `COUPON_TBD_PANEL` (legacy gyroid seat, now unused).

Once §5.1 is filled, every one of these gets a measured value and the press-fit section
print is the first thing on the bed.
