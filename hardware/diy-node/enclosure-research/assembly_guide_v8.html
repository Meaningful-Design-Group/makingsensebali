Steps 1–3  ·  Mount the body
You need: drill, screwdriver, the two wall screws  ·  body goes on the wall before any electronics go in
2
1
Mark 2 holes on the wall, 32 mm apart, horizontal.
Drill + set wall anchors. Screws flush, heads out ~4 mm.
2
Align the two keyholes over the screw heads.
Push body flat to wall, then slide down until screws seat in the narrow slot. Tug gently — it should not lift off.
3
From inside the body, slide the gyroid panel into the rebate in the left side wall (Plus). It snaps into the ledge — no glue, no hardware. Basic: front wall.

Steps 4–6  ·  Load the electronics
No tools needed for any of these steps  ·  ★ = Plus only  ·  Chimney runs BME680 low (intake) → XIAO high (exhaust)

4
★ Plus only
Slide HM3301 down the two front rails until the 2 pegs click into the floor holes. Fan grille faces forward. Carrier flat against floor.
⚠ can air inlet faces up inside bay — correct
5
Tilt perfboard into cavity and seat on the 4 back-wall standoffs. XIAO at top (exhaust zone), BME680 at bottom (intake zone). No screws — friction hold for now.
BME680 must face the gyroid window
6
Thread USB-C through the angled bottom chase. Form a drip loop — the cable must dip below the entry point before rising to the power supply. This keeps water from tracking in.

Steps 7–9  ·  Close the cap
No tools needed  ·  The bayonet is print-in-place — 3 lugs, 3 tabs, ~37° twist to lock
4
7
Press the insect mesh disc into the cap's central window. It should sit flush. The disc faces outward when the cap is installed.
8
Rotate the cap until the 3 lugs line up with the 3 gaps between the socket tabs. Push straight up until the disc brim contacts the body.
⚠ do not force — if it resists, re-align
9
Twist the cap ~37° clockwise (viewed from below). The lugs slide under the tabs and the cap cannot pull out. You should feel a slight resistance.
⚠ first-print: try lightly — COUPON_TBD clearances may need one calibration
Fully installed. Body on wall · gyroid panel seated · electronics loaded · USB-C drip loop down · cap locked.
Verify: tug cap — should not pull straight out. Cable has a U-loop before it rises. Gyroid panel sits flush, no gap at rim.

Unhook from keyholes (lift up, pull away)
On the bench: 2× M3 release the crown
Access perfboard + BME680 + HM3301
Re-hang as Steps 1–6
Firmware note:
BME680 reads ~1–2°C high from self-heating.
Apply T-offset in firmware; do NOT re-position
the sensor closer to the panel — the 3 mm air

Airflow logic (passive chimney):  Intake enters through the bottom cap mesh → rises through cavity past BME680 → warm air from XIAO exits through top exhaust slots. BME680 T/RH samples the incoming (cooler) air. Plus only: HM3301 has its own dedicated PM duct on the front face — that air path never mixes with the BME680 air.
